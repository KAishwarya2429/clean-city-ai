# Problem Statement

## Background

Urban waste management remains one of the biggest challenges faced by cities worldwide. Rapid urbanization, increasing population, and growing waste generation have made traditional waste collection systems inefficient and difficult to manage.

In many cities, citizens frequently experience:

- Missed garbage collection
- Overflowing public dustbins
- Illegal waste dumping
- Lack of transparency in complaint resolution
- Difficulty contacting responsible authorities
- Limited visibility into municipal operations

These issues contribute to:

- Environmental pollution
- Public health risks
- Poor city cleanliness
- Citizen dissatisfaction
- Increased operational costs for municipalities

## Existing Challenges

### For Citizens

- No centralized platform for reporting waste-related issues
- Limited tracking of complaint status
- Poor communication with garbage collection teams

### For Municipal Authorities

- Manual complaint handling
- Lack of real-time monitoring
- Inefficient route planning
- Limited waste analytics

### For the Environment

- Overflowing bins
- Increased landfill waste
- Illegal dumping
- Reduced recycling participation

## Proposed Solution

CleanCity AI is an AI and IoT-powered Smart Waste Management Platform designed to connect citizens, garbage collection workers, smart dustbins, and municipal authorities through a unified digital ecosystem.

The platform aims to improve waste collection efficiency, increase citizen participation, provide real-time monitoring, and support data-driven decision making.

## Goals

- Improve cleanliness of cities
- Reduce waste collection delays
- Increase transparency and accountability
- Enable smart waste monitoring
- Promote sustainable waste management practices
- Support Smart City initiatives

## Vision

To build a cleaner, smarter, and more sustainable urban environment through technology-driven waste management.
